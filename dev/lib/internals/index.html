BondGraphToolkit.bond_ports — Method
bond_ports()

The ports and bond variables of an element: power, the incoming port (its bond points into the element), pout, the outgoing port (its bond points out of it), and the bond's effort e and flow f along its half-arrow, whichever port carries it. Returns (power, pout, e, f, eqs). An element connects one of the two ports; MTK zeroes the flow of the other, so f = power.f - pout.f is the bond flow either way. MTK leaves the unused port's effort free, so it is tied to the bond's: both ports share one effort, and only the flow carries the direction.
